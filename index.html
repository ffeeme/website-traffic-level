How to use the Traffic Level Checker

This tool is designed for indie developers, bloggers, and site owners who want a quick, honest reality check on where their website stands.

  1. Enter your daily pageviews. Use the "Pageviews" metric from your analytics dashboard (not Sessions). AdSense RPM is calculated based on Pageviews, which is the official metric Google uses.
  2. Select the RPM that matches your traffic. RPM (Revenue Per Mille) is what you earn per 1,000 ad impressions. If your traffic is mostly from the US in finance, pick the high-value tier. If your audience is global and content is entertainment, pick the lower tier.
  3. Click Check My Level. You'll see a traffic tier with real-world examples, plus a revenue estimate in USD and CNY.
  4. Read the warning, not just the number. The revenue figure assumes each pageview loads an ad. If your pages have low engagement, ads may never be seen. The estimate is an optimistic ceiling, not a promise.
